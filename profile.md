## 👋 Hi, I'm Avery

**Product Manager** with an architecture background (Edinburgh MSc · NTUST CS minor) who also ships the code.

Architecture taught constraint satisfaction. PM work taught product judgment. The coding habit never left — which means I write the spec, build parts of it myself, and then configure HomeAssistant at 2am because the apartment is also a product. The main difference from designing buildings: iterations don't cost six months.

> Currently: AI carbon tracking platform · AI occupational health SaaS (GBZ 188-2025) · atomic UI library @ Shanghai  
> Also: 132 repos, an LLM-orchestrated personal OS, HomeAssistant pipelines, ESP32 talking to Claude.
