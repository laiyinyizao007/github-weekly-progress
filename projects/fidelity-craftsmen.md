## Overview

Fidelity Craftsmen is an AI-driven occupational health management platform built to comply with the Chinese national standard GBZ 188-2025. It streamlines the full lifecycle of workplace health surveillance — from risk assessment and medical examination scheduling to report generation and regulatory filing.

## Key Features

- GBZ 188-2025 compliant examination item catalogue and scheduling engine
- AI-assisted occupational exposure assessment and risk categorisation
- Automated health surveillance report generation
- Employee health record management with longitudinal tracking
- Integration with third-party medical institutions
