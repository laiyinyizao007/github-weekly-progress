## 👋 Hi, I'm Avery

Trained as an **architect** (Edinburgh MSc · NTUST CS minor). Now I build software.

The pivot made sense, actually — both disciplines are just obsessing over structure, constraints, and making things that don't fall apart when humans touch them. The main difference is buildings compile slower and you can't hotfix them in prod.

> Currently shipping AI-powered carbon tracking + health systems @ Shanghai.
> Side quests: Raspberry Pi at 2am, MCP servers, second brains.
